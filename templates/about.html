About us

We’re Sonya and Brandon. In conversations with Baltimoreans, we kept hearing about people making the trip to get help, only to find the doors closed or learn that the program couldn’t help with what they needed. Other people told us they didn’t know where to go in the first place.

We also saw how quickly funding could change what a program was able to offer. We started Baltimore Lifeline because we wanted people to have a clearer picture of where to go and what to expect before making the trip. Baltimore is where we started. As we expand to other communities, we’re carrying that work forward as LumaReach.
